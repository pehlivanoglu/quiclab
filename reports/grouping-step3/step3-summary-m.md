Pair decisions as ECG: SAME = both members (E >= b) of a common group, DIFFERENT = both members of some group but none in common, UNCERTAIN otherwise; truth = same leaf (ECG's truth.json pairs_leaf). 'every close' = pairs pooled over all event closes of the run; 'last' = after the last event. ECG = v1.2, delta 0.15 s, v2-owd onsets, its own event closes.
