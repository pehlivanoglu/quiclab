## 5. Verdicts (analyst)

- **Loss as a fraction over a short window works.** F5 rate 100 ms on the freeze set: theta 0.1 = precision 0.94,
  recall 0.80, 0.68 FA/min, p50 465 ms; theta 0.2 = 0.98 / 0.77 / 0.21 / 516 ms. v1 loss_only: 0.80 / 0.86 /
  2.66 / 419 ms. At a quarter of loss_only's false-alarm rate it loses 0.06-0.09 recall and ~50-100 ms; the
  pass check at loss_only's FA rate fails narrowly (recall -0.05, p90 +224 ms) because the curve stops at
  theta 0.1 (0.68 FA/min) and never needs loss_only's 2.66. Held-out AQMs: 0.90 / 0.83 / 1.04 at theta 0.1.
  Shorter window is better: 100 ms beats 200 / 300 ms at every theta (faster, and its keyframe peak p99 is 0.06
  vs 0.40 / 0.66).
- **Scaling the log-likelihood (x0.1, x0.3) changes nothing**: rows identical to the spec F5. G_ref is fitted per
  variant, so a constant factor scales G and G_ref together and the score G / G_ref is unchanged. Any coefficient
  in front of a single CUSUM is absorbed by the fitted scale; only the *relative* weight of features matters,
  and that is Step 4's fusion question.
- **Capping the count at 3 lost per tick is not enough**: keyframe peak score p50 still 1.00, calm090 worst flow
  mean 0.85, recall 0.16. The asymmetry remains: a tick with losses adds up to 3 x log(p1/p0) ~ +9, a clean tick
  removes ~0.3, so a few keyframe drops keep G high for seconds.
- Why the rate form fixes it: z-normalising the loss fraction bounds every tick's contribution to
  delta (z_max - delta/2) = +6 and gives clean ticks a full -2 (zc = 0), the same symmetric budget as F1-F3; a
  keyframe drop (10-50 packets in one tick) is diluted over the 100 ms window and clipped.
- Proposal for Step 4: replace the spec's per-tick LLR by "F5 rate 100 ms" (loss fraction over 3 ticks, EWMA z,
  floor 0.1 %), keep the F2 / F3 gate. Deviation from spec section 8; the LLR's theoretical optimality assumes
  independent Bernoulli loss, which keyframe drop bursts violate.
