## 5. Verdicts (analyst)

- **F1 (delay level) alone is the strongest single CUSUM.** On the freeze set at theta_on = 1.0: precision 0.98,
  recall 0.78, 0.17 FA/min, latency after t_on p50 395 ms, p90 625 ms. v1's full 4-signal detector on the same
  runs and truth: 0.99 / 0.89 / 0.11 / 367 / 794. One feature already matches v1's precision, beats its p90
  by 170 ms and loses 0.11 of recall. v1 has no single-signal counterpart (its level trigger is off), so no
  pass check; held-out AQMs: 0.97 / 0.78 / 0.25 / 395 / 670 (fq_codel brings the FA rate up, as Step 2 predicted).
- **F2 vs gradient_only: FAIL on recall, large win on latency.** At the matched 0.06 FA/min (theta 0.35):
  recall 0.55 vs 0.64, p50 222 vs 992 ms, p90 467 vs 1652 ms. The CUSUM reaches the same evidence 4x earlier
  but the clipped z (|z| <= 4) and the drift back to zero give up on slow onsets that v1's 1 s trendline
  still catches; fusion with F1 is the intended remedy (Step 4).
- **F3 (one-GOP pending growth): PASS vs deficit_only, cannot reach backlog_only's or offered_only's FA rate.**
  Its lowest FA rate (theta 1.0) is 0.33/min with p50 830 ms: the 1 s window makes it slow and its calm tail
  keeps firing. It is a hold / confirmation feature, not an onset feature; consistent with Steps 1-2.
- **F5 (loss) as specified is unusable: recall 0.05-0.11.** Cause, visible in table 4 (keyframe peak score
  p50 = 1.00): one keyframe loss burst (10-50 packets at ~ +3 log-units each) saturates G at 2 G_ref, and the
  no-loss ticks drain it at only ~0.3 per tick, so the score stays at 1 for about a minute, no new alarm can
  start, and G_ref itself (289, fitted on calm065's real keyframe drops) is far above anything a congestion
  loss reaches in time. The spec's log-likelihood form is right for Bernoulli loss but the per-tick
  magnitude must be bounded (e.g. cap l_k at a few packets per tick, or score the loss *rate* over the
  1 s window instead of the count). Not changed here; proposal for Step 4.
- **Keyframe (decision 5):** peak score p99 in calm periods F1 0.60, F3 0.44, F2 0.15, F5 1.00. F1 crosses the
  0.5 line: at theta_on = 0.5 a keyframe can start an episode on its own (the 0.44 FA/min at theta 0.5 in
  table 2 are mostly these); at theta_on >= 0.8 they do not. The min-filter the decision prescribes was for
  F3 / F4 and does not apply to F1 (its tick minimum already is one); the remedy for F1 is theta_on, i.e. the
  operating point, which is the evaluator's parameter anyway.
- **calm090 (decision 2):** F1 mean score 0.11 (worst flow 0.69), F3 0.06, F2 0.01. The level feature does sit
  higher on a loaded-but-clear link, but at the theta_on values of interest (>= 0.5) the median flow does not alarm.
- **ProbeRTT reset: no effect** (identical rows for keep / reset on every feature): the probe_rtt ticks are masked
  and G is at or near zero when they end. Decision: keep G (simpler).
- **Theory vs measurement:** e^h predicts 10^8-10^10 ticks between false alarms for h = theta G_ref ~ 19-24;
  measured 2 000-33 000 ticks. The Gaussian-independence assumption is off by four to six orders of magnitude
  (heavy tails, GOP periodicity, autocorrelation); as the spec says, the number is shown and not used.
- Pass criterion ("curve not worse than the v1 *_only point"): **met for F3 vs deficit_only and, in latency, for F2;
  not met for F2 in recall and for F5 at all.** The decision gate is Step 4 (fusion at matched FA against v1's
  2-of-4); the F1 result makes that gate plausible, the F5 result needs the loss score bounded first.
