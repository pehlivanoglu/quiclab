## D1 (light health check): NOT accepted yet, because of a simulator recording fault it found

- **Negative tests: all three caught** (truncated sender file, missing counter row, wrong capacity x1.25).
- **Agreement with the frozen check:** the light check passes on 40 of the 44 B2 runs and on all regress-d1 runs.
  - It fails item 3 (service rate) on the 4 A4-hhier-codel runs: median busy rate / capacity is 1.055 on the leaf
    egresses, against a 1.05 limit.
  - On the regress-d1 sample run, the queue-disc totals equal tracer-counts.csv exactly.
- **Cause: the simulator's queue recorder double-counts CoDel drops** (ns-3 `queue-disc.cc:569, 687-704`). A packet
  that CoDel removes from its internal queue and then drops fires the queue disc's "Dequeue" trace as well as
  "DropAfterDequeue". quicsim's egress monitor therefore:
  - counts it as dequeued and transmitted (router-metrics transmitted_bytes too high under CoDel);
  - writes a "dequeue" row and a "drop" row for it in the router trace.

  Evidence:
  - tracer-counts enq - deq - drop = -16,902 / -37,832 / -31,422 on the three A4-codel egresses;
  - the frozen health records identity_ok = 0 on 8 B2 runs (A2-sburst-codel and A4-hhier-codel, both reps, both ACK
    profiles), but identity is information there, not a gate;
  - the testbed counterpart (K1 A4-hhier-codel-r1) has identity_ok = 1 everywhere;
  - calm CoDel runs (no drops after dequeue) are unaffected.
- **Not changed.** It affects the accepted B2 CoDel rows (GT-Q, router-metrics load / keyframe numbers in those
  cells). The fix would hold the dequeue record until it is known that the packet is not dropped, and undo the
  dequeue count on DropAfterDequeue. It changes the B2 CoDel outputs, so a decision is needed (U1).
